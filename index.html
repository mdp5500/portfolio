Composer, guitarist, and conductor working between song and score.

Mat Di Pizzo writes music that sits at the crossing of French chanson, bossa nova, West African rhythm, and New Orleans blues — a language built for voice, nylon guitar, and a light hand of electronics.

Alongside the live project, Mat composes for the stage: scoring credits include Le Dieu du Carnage and Wagon 32. Formal training runs through conducting and orchestration, with an ongoing practice in tonal harmony, arrangement, and live looping.

Currently based between Mendoza, Montréal, and France — open to scoring work, commissions, and collaboration.
